Privacy & responsible use

  • IP addresses entered for lookup are sent to ipwho.is.
  • Phone numbers are parsed locally and are not sent to a phone lookup service.
  • History is stored in the local geotrace.db SQLite file.
  • Only public, globally routable IP addresses are accepted.
  • No live tracking, covert surveillance, or carrier-system access is included.
